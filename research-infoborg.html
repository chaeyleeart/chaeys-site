Infoborg

Digital Capitalism and the ‘Infoborg’ — Ontological Experiments in Contemporary Media Art

디지털 자본주의와 ‘인포보그’ : 동시대 미디어아트의 존재 실험

Chaeyoung Lee · M.A. in Art Theory
Department of Art Studies, Hongik University

Abstract · Summary

In my 2025 master’s thesis, I proposed “Infoborg” to examine how emotion, memory, and the body become data within cycles of extraction, computation, and self-reference. Drawing on posthuman theory, cybernetics, biomedia, and technological ontology, I analysed works by Weidi Zhang and Rodger Luo, Pierre Huyghe, Ian Cheng, LuYang, and Refik Anadol. The thesis considered how these works give form to information-based subjectivity, and explored questions of control, resistance, and self-formation under digital capitalism.
