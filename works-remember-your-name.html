YARR Studio × 8akeryy · Team project

I’ll Remember Your Name기다릴게, 이름을

2026 · AI animation · Colour, sound · 2 min 31 sec

At a bakery by the rainbow bridge, Bang-i bakes cakes bearing the names of the families the dogs are waiting to meet. When a visitor leaves the name field blank, Bang-i makes a cake with the love remembered from home. As the arrival board changes, Bang-i prepares for a reunion of their own.

Complete film · Korean narration with Korean and English subtitles
